Who Am I? — Privacy Policy Effective Date: October 24, 2026 1. Introduction Who Am I? ("the Game") is a multiplayer guessing game designed for use through Discord. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when participants use the Game. 2. Information We Collect Depending on the features enabled, the Game may process: Discord information: Discord user IDs, usernames, and other basic account information needed to identify participants. Gameplay information: Player participation, assigned characters, scores, rounds, and game settings. Submitted content: Character names, images, categories, tags, and other content submitted to the Game. Technical information: Basic connection information and server logs necessary for operation, security, and troubleshooting. The Game does not request Discord passwords or payment card information. 3. How We Use Information Information is used to: Identify players and manage multiplayer sessions. Display character assignments and gameplay information. Track scores and manage rounds. Store and display approved character content. Maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the Game. 4. Information may also be processed by infrastructure providers used to host or operate the Game. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. 5. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, maintain its functionality, or meet applicable legal obligations. Information may be deleted when it is no longer needed or upon a valid deletion request, subject to applicable requirements. 6. Data Security Reasonable measures are taken to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, and loss. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information. 7. Your Privacy Rights Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable law and technical limitations.
